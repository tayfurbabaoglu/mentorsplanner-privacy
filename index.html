MentorsPlanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

MentorsPlanner ("the app") helps independent tutors track their lessons, payments and their students' progress. MentorsPlanner is developed by Tayfur Babaoglu.

What data the app handles

Everything you enter (student names, phone numbers and e-mail addresses, lesson dates and fees, payments, notes, goals and lesson evaluations) is stored only on your device.

  • The app has no server; this information is never sent to us or to third parties.
  • No account is required.
  • The app shows no ads, uses no advertising ID and collects no usage analytics.

Backups and sharing

  • Backup file: "Create backup file" writes all your records to a single file. You decide where to keep it (for example Google Drive or e-mail); those services' own privacy terms apply.

  • Sharing messages: When you share a payment reminder or an account statement, the text is passed to the app you choose in your phone's share menu (WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail, …). This only happens when you ask for it.

  • PDF documents: Statement and receipt PDFs are created on your device and shared only with the app you choose.

Notifications

Lesson reminders and summaries are scheduled locally on your device. Their content never passes through a server.

Store rating

After some use, the app may once open the store's own rating dialog (Google Play / App Store). The store runs that dialog; the app sends no information about you or your records.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play / the App Store. RevenueCat is used to verify the subscription status. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous app user ID and purchase information, never your student, lesson or payment records. Your payment details never reach the app.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you can delete it inside the app at any time, or remove it completely by uninstalling the app. Make a backup before uninstalling; otherwise the data cannot be recovered.

Children

The app is designed for teachers and is not directed at anyone under 18. You are responsible for the information you enter about your students; it never leaves your device.
