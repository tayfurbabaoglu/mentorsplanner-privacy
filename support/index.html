MentorsPlanner Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to mentorsplanner.support@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. MentorsPlanner has no server and needs no account. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone open Settings > Data > Create backup file and keep the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive or e-mail). On the new phone install MentorsPlanner and use Settings > Data > Restore from backup.

What does the free version include?

Lessons, payments, balances, finance, reminders and backups, for up to two active students. Archived students do not count. Your records are never locked, with or without a subscription.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then open Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store:

  • iPhone: Settings app > your name > Subscriptions > MentorsPlanner.
  • Android: Google Play > profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > MentorsPlanner.

Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your records stay as they are.
